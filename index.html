Our Love Story

1

💼 A simple first meeting

In January 2025, Wahyu, a 28-year-old graphic designer, met Riski, a thoughtful, reserved copywriter, while working on a company branding project. Their meetings were courteous and professional. Wahyu had no idea that Riski had seen and admired his work at a national competition two years earlier. She kept her distance and focused on the project.

2

💞 Love takes root in a difficult time

In February 2025, the client rejected the initial concept and threatened to cancel the contract. Riski found a creative solution that saved the project. Wahyu was struck by her composure and intelligence. Her posts about faith and patience made him feel they shared the same values. After praying for guidance, he asked a trusted religious teacher to introduce them formally for marriage. Riski told him she had noticed him for some time too, and had been waiting for a clear intention.

3

💍 A step toward Allah’s blessing

With a chaperone’s guidance, Wahyu and Riski spoke openly about building a family with Allah’s blessing. Riski’s family worried about Wahyu’s modest means. When he shared his plans for a community arts enterprise he had been developing, they saw his commitment to serving others and gave their blessing. In March 2025, after another prayer for guidance, Wahyu proposed in a simple, meaningful gathering. They set out toward marriage trusting that Allah had brought them together.
